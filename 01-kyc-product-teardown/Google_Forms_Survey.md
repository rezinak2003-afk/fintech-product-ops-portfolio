# Google Forms Survey — Copy-ready draft

**Form title:** Identity verification usability feedback (fictional prototype)

**Intro/consent:** “This short survey asks about a fictional onboarding prototype. Do not submit real identity numbers, bank details, passwords, one-time passwords, or identity documents. Participation is voluntary. Responses will be used for a student portfolio exercise. [Add your contact, retention period, and any required consent/privacy wording before publishing.]”

1. **Did you complete the sample verification flow?** (Multiple choice: Yes, without help / Yes, with help / No)
2. **How clear were the document requirements before upload?** (Linear scale 1 = Not at all clear, 5 = Extremely clear)
3. **How confident did you feel about what would happen after submission?** (Linear scale 1–5)
4. **Which step was hardest?** (Multiple choice: Understanding eligibility / Entering details / Choosing a document / Upload/recovery / Reviewing / None)
5. **Did you encounter an error or need to go back?** (Yes / No / Not sure)
6. **What information or help would have made the task easier?** (Paragraph, optional; reminder not to include sensitive information)
7. **What device did you use?** (Mobile / Desktop / Tablet / Prefer not to say)
8. **Anything else you would change?** (Paragraph, optional)

## Configure and share
1. Create a Blank Google Form at https://forms.google.com.
2. Add the introduction and questions above; mark only the minimum necessary fields required.
3. Link responses to a dedicated Google Sheet and restrict editor access to people who need it.
4. Preview on mobile and desktop; ensure the consent wording and privacy settings are appropriate.
5. Publish/share the responder link, then replace the placeholder in the project page with the public responder URL if desired.

Do not describe survey responses as collected until responses have actually been received and reviewed.
