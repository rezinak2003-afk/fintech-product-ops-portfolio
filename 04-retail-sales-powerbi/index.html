Methodology, lineage & limitations

Designed to be auditable and reproducible

Dataset

UCI Online Retail II is a two-year transaction dataset from a UK-based non-store online retailer. This dashboard covers transactions from December 2009 to 9 December 2011; the final month is incomplete.

Official UCI dataset · CC BY 4.0 · DOI 10.24432/C5CG6D

Revenue definition

Published aggregate sales outputs define revenue as quantity × unit price for positive, valid sale rows after cleaning. Cancellation/return lines are analysed separately. Currency is GBP.

Customer analysis

RFM segments are based on customer recency, invoice frequency and monetary value. Cohort month is the month of a customer's first identified purchase. Cohort retention requires an identified customer.

Data lineage

Precomputed CSV extracts are adapted from public aggregate outputs hosted in the ndkn-code/online-retail-analytics repository. The original workbook is not bundled. This project does not claim to have independently recomputed those source aggregates.

Quality caveats

  • Historic data, not current business performance.
  • December 2011 is partial; monthly comparisons should account for exposure days.
  • Customer-level analysis excludes records without customer identifiers.
  • Published aggregate metrics can vary under different cleaning rules.
