# Insights & business recommendations

The observations below describe the **included published aggregate extracts** from UCI Online Retail II and are not current market evidence.

## 1. Revenue seasonality
The monthly series peaks in November in both observed years. November 2011 records about £1.45M, while November 2010 records about £1.43M. This is consistent with pre-holiday sales concentration, but the dashboard alone does not establish causality.

**Recommendation:** plan stock and campaign readiness ahead of Q4, then validate seasonality over more years before setting targets.

## 2. Country concentration
The United Kingdom represents about 85.5% of included net revenue. EIRE, the Netherlands, Germany and France are the leading markets after the UK.

**Recommendation:** test market-specific acquisition and fulfilment economics in the largest non-UK markets rather than expanding indiscriminately.

## 3. Product mix
The leading product by value in the published aggregate extract is REGENCY CAKESTAND 3 TIER (about £330.6K). The next two listed products are WHITE HANGING HEART T-LIGHT HOLDER and RED RETROSPOT JUMBO BAG.

**Recommendation:** pair revenue rank with margin, stock-outs and return rates before interpreting product performance as profitability.

## 4. Retention & customer value
The public analysis reports that the Champions segment is 24.9% of identified customers and contributes 68.8% of identified-customer revenue; Loyal Customers contribute a further 14.6%. Month-one retention varies substantially by cohort.

**Recommendation:** test differentiated loyalty / win-back journeys and measure incremental repeat orders with holdout groups; segment association alone is not causal evidence.

## 5. Returns
The monthly return ratio has visible spikes, including periods at the beginning and end of the observed window. December 2011 is a partial month through 9 December, so its apparent spike needs exposure and cut-off context.

**Recommendation:** investigate cancellation reason codes and product-level return share, and avoid comparing partial months with full months without normalisation.

## Scope caveats
The aggregates are adapted with attribution from a public analysis repository. This project does not bundle the full raw workbook; its included pipeline is provided for independent reprocessing. Cleaning rules determine totals and should be checked before comparing to other analyses.
